Coffee story

Origin-led Indian coffee with a marketable flavor identity.

Our coffee comes from Karnataka’s well-known coffee belts, especially Chikmagalur and Coorg, where altitude, shade-grown cultivation, and estate-style processing create a profile that works for both specialty positioning and large-scale commercial use. Arabica offers a smoother cup with refined acidity and aroma, while Robusta provides stronger body, deeper intensity, and blending strength.

  • Arabica: smoother mouthfeel, refined aroma, balanced acidity
  • Robusta: heavier body, stronger crema potential, bold commercial profile
  • Flavor profile: cocoa notes, nutty sweetness, mild spice, earthy depth depending on grade and process
