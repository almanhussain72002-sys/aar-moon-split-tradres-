Freshness story

Vegetable sourcing built around speed, sorting, and shelf performance.

Fresh vegetables are only competitive when timing is controlled. We focus on fast post-harvest movement, practical sorting, and packing decisions that reduce handling stress and help importers receive produce with stronger visual quality and shelf-life potential.

  • Sourcing regions: Karnataka and nearby South Indian growing belts with reliable produce access
  • Freshness approach: daily harvest alignment and quick movement to grading and packing points
  • Market fit: suitable for wholesale, retail, and supermarket replenishment programs
Quality and grading

Produce quality is measured by how it arrives and sells.

Fresh handling

  • Packing selected to improve transit resilience and arrival quality
