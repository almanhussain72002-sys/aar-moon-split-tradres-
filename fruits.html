Freshness story

Fruit exports are won through timing, ripeness control, and visual appeal.

For tropical fruits, quality depends on the right harvest stage, protective packing, and export planning that keeps the product attractive at arrival. We focus on matching fruit condition to buyer expectations in wholesale and retail markets across the Gulf.

  • Sourcing regions: Karnataka and South Indian fruit belts based on season and product type
  • Freshness logic: harvest maturity and packing selected according to market destination
  • Quality grading: size, color, surface appearance, and sellable presentation are prioritized
Product and grading overview

A fruit program should be built for how it sells, not just how it looks at origin.
